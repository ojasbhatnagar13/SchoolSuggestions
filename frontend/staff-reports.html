Speak Up reports.

What students have told staff about something wrong. Never shown to students. Urgent ones come first.

Students use Speak Up to tell staff about something wrong. Most are anonymous: nobody, admins included, can find out who sent them. Some include the student's name and email so you can talk to them. Reports are not checked for truth, so treat them as a lead, not proof.
