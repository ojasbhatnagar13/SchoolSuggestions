Privacy

What we keep,
and who sees it.

DPSI School Suggestions is a student-made suggestion box for DPS International Edge. Staff never see who sent a suggestion.

Privacy policy

In plain words.

Last updated 20 November 2026

Signing in

You sign in with your Google school account. We receive your email address and name from Google. We use them only to check that you're a DPSI student and to keep your votes and suggestions working. We never see your Google password.

What we store

  • Your suggestions, with the time rounded to the hour. They are linked to your account so you can follow them on My suggestions. Staff can never see which account sent a suggestion.
  • Your grade and section, only if you choose them when sending a suggestion. Your name and email are never attached to a suggestion for staff to see, only your section is used, and only to count your class's total. Staff never see the section on your suggestion either: the leaderboard shows totals only.
  • Speak Up reports, without anything about your account, so nobody can find out who sent one. Only if you switch on "Include my name" is your email stored with it, and then staff see it so they can talk to you. Reports are only seen by staff.
  • Your votes, so you can only vote once and can take a vote back.
  • Feedback about the site, if you send any.
  • A scrambled code to limit how many suggestions an account can send in an hour. It can't be turned back into your account and is deleted after two days.

Who can see it

  • Staff see the text of suggestions but never who sent them. They don't see who voted for what either.
  • Other students only see suggestions after staff approve them, with no names attached. Vote counts are never shown to students.
  • The site's admin can technically reach the database to keep the site running and fix problems, and does not look at who sent what.

The AI check

The text of each suggestion is sent to Google's Gemini AI to check it against the school guidelines. Your name and email are not sent with it. While the site uses Gemini's free service, Google may use what is sent to improve its products, so please don't put personal details in a suggestion.

Services we use

Supabase stores the data and handles sign-in. Cloudflare hosts the website. Google provides sign-in and the AI check. We don't sell any data, show ads, or use tracking cookies.

Getting your data removed

Ask a teacher, or contact the site's maker, Ojas Bhatnagar, and your account, votes and linked suggestions will be deleted.
