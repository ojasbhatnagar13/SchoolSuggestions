Anonymous by design.
Decided by people.

A paper suggestion box has three problems. Ideas go in and nobody hears back. The same request arrives twenty times. And someone has to read every slip before any real work can start.

This fixes all three without taking people out of the decision. The AI sorts and filters. Staff decide what the school actually does.

How it works

The AI sorts.
People decide.

What the AI checks

  • Whether it breaks a school guideline, with the guideline quoted back to you
  • Whether it's spam, abuse, or an attempt to trick the system
  • Whether the same idea has already been suggested, however it's worded
  • A category and a one-line summary, so staff can read the queue quickly

What only people decide

  • Whether an idea is approved and shown to other students
  • Overturning any automatic decision the AI got wrong
  • What the school actually acts on, and when
  • Anything that needs judgement the guidelines don't cover

Questions

Before you submit.

Is it really anonymous?

Yes. Suggestions are stored with no name, email or account. To stop spam, the site keeps a scrambled fingerprint of your connection for two days, and it can't be linked back to what you wrote.

Why was my suggestion declined?

It broke one of the guidelines above, and you'll have been told which one. If you think the AI got it wrong, reword it or speak to a teacher. Staff can see declined suggestions and overturn them.

Why can't I see my suggestion yet?

Nothing appears on the board until a member of staff approves it. That's deliberate: every public idea has been read by a person first.

It said my idea already exists.

Someone suggested the same thing, possibly in different words. Rather than filing it twice, you're shown the original, which you can back once it's on the board.

Why sign in to see the board?

So the list stays inside the school instead of being shared around online. Submitting never needs an account.

Is there a limit on how many I can send?

A few an hour from each device, to stop spam. If you reach it, wait a while and try again. It resets on its own.
