Who does what

What the AI does,
and what it never does.

What the AI checks

  • Whether it breaks a school guideline, with the guideline quoted back to you
  • Whether it's a real suggestion at all, or spam, abuse, or an attempt to trick the system
  • Whether the same idea has already been suggested, however it's worded
  • A category and a one-line summary, so staff can read the queue quickly

What only people decide

  • Whether an idea is approved and shown to other students
  • Overturning any automatic decision the AI got wrong
  • What the school actually acts on, and when
  • Anything that needs judgement the guidelines don't cover

Questions

Before you send one.

Is it really anonymous?

Yes. You sign in, but the idea itself is stored with no name, email or account. To count how many you've sent, the site keeps a scrambled code for your account and the hour you sent it, for two days. Staff never see it, and it isn't stored with your idea.

Can I report bullying or a problem here?

Please don't. Talk to a teacher or a school counsellor in person. This box is anonymous, so nobody can reply to you, check you're okay, or help you through it. If something is sent here anyway, staff see it straight away and pass it to the pastoral care team.

Why was my idea turned down?

Either it broke one of the guidelines above, or it didn't read as a real suggestion. You'll have been told which. If the AI got it wrong, reword it or speak to a teacher. Staff see everything the AI turns down and can overturn it.

Why can't I see my idea yet?

Nothing appears on the Ideas page until a member of staff approves it. That's deliberate: every idea other students see has been read by a person first.

It said my idea already exists.

Someone suggested the same thing, possibly in different words. Rather than filing it twice, you're shown the original, which you can back once it's on the Ideas page.

Why do I have to sign in?

So only students can send ideas and see the list, and so nobody can flood the box by opening a private window or switching network. Google also proves the account is real.

Is there a limit on how many I can send?

Five an hour and twenty a day for each student, to stop spam. If you reach it, wait a while and try again. It resets on its own.
