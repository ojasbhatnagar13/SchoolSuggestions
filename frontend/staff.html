How to review

Five states.
You can change every one.

Pending

Waiting for you. Anything the AI rated Feasible or Needs Review lands here, and it's shown first.

Approved

On the students' Ideas page, where they can back it with one vote each.

Actioned

The school has acted on it. Kept on record so nothing is quietly forgotten.

Rejected

A real idea that breaks a guideline. Hidden from students, and from this page unless you tick it.

Spam

Not a genuine suggestion: jokes, nonsense, abuse, tricks. Hidden the same way.

Every card says who made the call. AI means it was sorted automatically when it arrived. Staff means a person chose the status, and the card names the account. The AI's own verdict is always shown too, so you can see where you and it disagreed.
