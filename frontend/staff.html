How to review

Four states.
You set every one.

Pending

Waiting for a decision. Anything the AI rated Feasible or Needs Review lands here.

Approved

Shown on the student board, where students can back it with one vote each.

Rejected

Hidden from students. AI-declined suggestions arrive here. Move one back if the AI got it wrong.

Actioned

The school has acted on it. Stays on record so nothing is quietly forgotten.
