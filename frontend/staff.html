Change these and the very next suggestion is judged by the new version. The guidelines are shown to students on the How it works page. The background is only for the AI.

A heading ending in a colon, then one rule per line starting with "- ". Headings containing "not" show red, "review" amber, anything else green. Only these can make the AI turn an idea down.

Facts that help the AI judge ideas: facilities, food, transport, how things work here. It flags clashes for you to review, but never turns an idea down on these alone.

How to review

Five states.
You can change every one.

Pending

Waiting for you. Anything the AI rated Feasible or Needs Review lands here, and it's shown first.

Approved

On the students' Ideas page, where they can back it with one vote each.

Actioned

The school has acted on it. Students see it on the Ideas page marked Done, so they know suggestions lead somewhere.

Rejected

A real idea that breaks a guideline. Hidden from students, and from this page unless you tick it.

Spam

Not a genuine suggestion: jokes, nonsense, abuse, tricks. Hidden the same way.

Every card says who made the call. AI means it was sorted automatically when it arrived. Staff means a person chose the status, and the card names the account. The AI's own verdict is always shown too, so you can see where you and it disagreed.
